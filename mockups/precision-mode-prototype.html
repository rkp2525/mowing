Hold Shift for Precision mode

Does a slow, full-circle pivot with a visible cutting line make careful edging feel easy? Drive the mower below. This is an isolated mock with no game saves.

⇧ Shift = Precision mode
Normal modeMovement: 100%Facing: NorthCutting guide: hiddenMowed: 0%Flowers clipped: 0

Normal: W A S D / arrows move across the lawn, or click and hold to drive toward the pointer. Hold Shift for Precision mode: A/D or ←/→ slowly pivot in place through the full 360°. W/S or ↑/↓ move forward/backward along your chosen angle. Combine them for a gentle curve. Space pauses · R resets.

Hold Shift to reveal your cutting line.
White dashed line = the middle of your next pass. Pale rails = the full cutting width.Hold Shift + left/right to pivot. Add up/down when you are lined up.
